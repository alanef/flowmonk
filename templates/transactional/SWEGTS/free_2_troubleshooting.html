If Emails Are Still Going to Spam

The plugin has built-in diagnostics. Go to the settings page and check:

  1. SPF check – does your domain's SPF record include your server's IP?
  2. Blacklist check – is your server's IP on any spam blacklists?

If your server IP is blacklisted, you'll need to either:

  • Contact your hosting provider to get it delisted
  • Switch to an SMTP plugin that sends via a clean relay

Common Settings to Try

  • Sender email – use an address on your actual domain, not a generic one
  • Sender name – change "WordPress" to your site name or your own name

Still Stuck?

Drop a message in the support forum with details about your hosting setup. Email deliverability can be frustrating, but there's usually a fix.
