A Few Tips

Test it yourself first

PayPal has a sandbox mode for testing. Or just make a small payment to yourself and refund it – worth doing before going live.

Multiple forms

You can create different forms for different purposes. Each form has its own name and settings. Use [qpp form="formname"] to display a specific one.

Payment records

The plugin keeps a log of all payments. Go to Quick PayPal Payments → Payment Records to download them as a CSV.

IPN (Instant Payment Notification)

If you need to trigger actions when payments come in, enable IPN in your PayPal account settings. The plugin receives these notifications automatically.

Common Use Cases

  • Donations with suggested amounts
  • Simple product sales without WooCommerce overhead
  • Service deposits or consultations
  • Invoice payments with reference numbers
