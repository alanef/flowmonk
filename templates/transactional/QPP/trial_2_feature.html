Have you tried the multiple products feature yet?

How It Works

Instead of selling one thing at a time, you can list up to 9 products in a single form. Each one has:

  • A name
  • A price
  • A checkbox for selection

Customers tick what they want, the form calculates the total, and they pay in one transaction.

Use Cases

  • Service packages: "Basic", "Standard", "Premium" with add-ons
  • Event tickets: Different ticket types or merchandise
  • Consulting services: Different session lengths or extras
  • Donations: Suggested amounts with an "other" option

How to Set It Up

Go to Quick PayPal Payments → Settings and look for the "Products" section. Add your items, set prices, and save. The form will show checkboxes automatically.
