Getting Started

  1. Go to Quick PayPal Payments → Settings
  2. Enter your PayPal email address
  3. Select your currency
  4. Add [qpp] to any page or post

That's the basics. Customers can now pay you directly from your WordPress site.

What You Can Do

  • Fixed amounts: Set a specific price for a product or service
  • Variable amounts: Let customers enter their own amount (donations, custom orders)
  • Reference numbers: Collect order IDs, invoice numbers, etc.
  • Quantity selection: Let customers choose how many they want

Customising the Form

In settings, you can:

  • Change all the form labels
  • Style colours, fonts, and borders
  • Add terms & conditions
  • Set up autoresponder emails
