Have you tried on-site checkout yet?

Why It Matters

Every time a visitor clicks through to Eventbrite to buy a ticket, you lose some of them. They get distracted, forget, or just don't want to leave your site.

On-site checkout keeps them on your page. The ticket purchase happens in a popup right there.

How to Enable It

Add popup="true" to your shortcode:

[wfea popup="true"]

That's it. Your "Buy Now" button now opens a checkout overlay instead of redirecting to Eventbrite.

More Checkout Options

Set your Buy Now text with event status, highly recommended you use this a it auto disables button that can not be used like Sold Out

  • sold_out_button=”Sold out”
  • postponed_button=”Postponed”
  • unavailable_button=”Unavailable”
  • coming_soon_button=”Coming soon”
  • past_event_button=”Ended”
  • canceled_event_button=”Canceled”
  • started_event_button=”Event started”
  • sales_ended_button=”Sales ended”
