The Quarantine

Go to Fullworks Anti-Spam → Quarantine. This shows every blocked submission – you can see exactly what's being caught and why.

If a legitimate email gets flagged (rare, but it happens), you can find it here and add the sender to your allow list.

Fine-Tuning with Block/Allow Lists

In Fullworks Anti-Spam → Settings, you'll find:

  • Allow list: Email addresses or domains that should always get through
  • Block list: IPs, email patterns, or keywords to always block

Useful if you have trusted partners who submit forms regularly, or if you're seeing spam with specific patterns.

Stats Page

The stats page shows the breakdown between bot detections and ML (human spam) detections. It's interesting to see how much human-generated spam you were getting that the free version wouldn't have caught.
