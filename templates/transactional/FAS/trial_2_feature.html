What It Does

The email quarantine shows you exactly what's being blocked and why. Go to Fullworks Anti-Spam → Quarantine in your dashboard.

You'll see:

  • Every blocked submission
  • Whether it was caught by bot detection or ML spam detection
  • The actual content of the spam
  • Option to whitelist if something was blocked incorrectly

Why It Matters

Most anti-spam plugins are a black box – something gets blocked and you never know if it was legitimate. The quarantine gives you visibility. If a real customer's email gets flagged, you can find it and add them to the allow list.

Fine-Tuning

The block/allow lists let you:

  • Allow specific email addresses or domains
  • Block specific IPs or IP ranges
  • Block by keyword patterns

Find these in Fullworks Anti-Spam → Settings.
