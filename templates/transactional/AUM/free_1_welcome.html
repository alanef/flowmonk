What You Get

  • Admin bar widget – quick count of online users
  • Dashboard widget – at-a-glance view of who's logged in
  • Users list enhancements – online status indicators and last-seen timestamps
  • Audit log – track login/logout events

WP-CLI Commands

For automation and scripting:

  • wp active-users list – list online users
  • wp active-users stats – show statistics
  • wp active-users any --quiet – check if anyone's online (for scripts)
  • wp active-users wait-clear – wait until no users are online

Useful for safe maintenance scripts – only update when nobody's using the site.
