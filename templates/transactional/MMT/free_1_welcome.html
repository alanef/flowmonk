Getting Started

  1. Go to Team Members in your dashboard
  2. Add your team members with photos, bios, and contact info
  3. Add the shortcode [meet-my-team] to any page

Shortcode Options

  • cols="3" – number of columns (1, 2, 3, 4, or 6)
  • enable_modal="true" – click to expand details in a popup
  • show_groups="Leadership" – filter by team group
  • align="center" – center align columns

Example:

[meet-my-team cols="4" enable_modal="true"]

The grid is responsive – it automatically shrinks to a single column on mobile.
