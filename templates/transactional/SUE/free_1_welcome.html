I'm Alan, the developer of Stop User Enumeration. Thanks for installing it.

Your site is now protected from one of the most common reconnaissance attacks hackers use. Before they try to brute-force your login, they probe for usernames. Now they can't.

What's Being Blocked

  • Author archive probes – requests like ?author=1
  • REST API user lookups – the /wp-json/wp/v2/users endpoint
  • Author sitemaps – WordPress 5.5+ exposes users in sitemaps by default
  • oEmbed data – can leak author info in embedded content

The plugin works automatically with sensible defaults. No configuration required.

One Thing to Check

If your WordPress display name is the same as your username, attackers can still find it in your posts and comments. Go to Users → Your Profile and make sure your Display Name is different from your login username.
